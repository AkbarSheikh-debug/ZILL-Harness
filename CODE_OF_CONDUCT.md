# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers, pledge to make participation in
ZILL Harness a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming to newcomers
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other channel), and when someone is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@AkbarSheikh-debug](https://github.com/AkbarSheikh-debug), or use GitHub's
"Report content" option. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly,
and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. They may also
warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
